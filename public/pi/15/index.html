Pinto Improvements

PI-15: Beanstalk Repayments and Bug Fixes

Executed on March 6, 2026 at 16:58 UTC.

Summary

  1. Beanstalk Repayments: Add separate repayment routes for Beanstalk's Field, Silo and Barn obligations, with transferable positions, contract-account claims and accounting hooks for Pinto shipments and Internal Balance transfers.
  1. Convert Accounting and Penalties: Fix capacity double-counting and normalize the Grown Stalk penalty to simulated liquidity impact.
  1. Referral Eligibility: Preserve a former delegate's independently earned eligibility when referral delegation changes, and reject delegation to the zero address.
  1. Plot Management: Add bounded queries of Plot indexes and combine adjacent Plots to simplify position management.

Background

Pinto committed at launch to start repaying Beanstalk’s obligations after supply exceeds 1 billion Pinto. The original writeup specifies Arbitrum block 276160746 for the holder snapshot, with Silo allocations based on fully recapitalized Unripe value, equivalent Field positions and Barn Fertilizer balances.

Problems

1. Beanstalk Repayments

The contracts and accounting needed to repay Beanstalk’s obligations had not yet been implemented.

2. Convert Accounting and Penalties

The per-block Convert capacity used to calculate Grown Stalk penalties tracks movement toward peg across all Wells and within each Well. Its accounting counted earlier Converts again with each new Convert in the same block, overstating capacity usage.

Grown Stalk penalties had two calculation problems. Overall movement toward or away from peg was measured from the spot reserve imbalance immediately before the Convert, making the result sensitive to preceding trades. The resulting penalty amount was then treated directly as Pinto Denominated Value (PDV) subject to Grown Stalk removal, rather than scaled to the Convert’s size and simulated effect on pool liquidity.

3. Referral Eligibility

Removing delegation from an account cleared the former delegate's eligibility, even when that account had independently Sown enough Pinto to qualify. The function also permitted delegation to the zero address.

4. Plot Management

The Field interface lacked bounded queries for positions in an account's Plot-index list and a way to combine adjacent Plots.

Solutions

1. Beanstalk Repayments

Add separate repayment positions for Beanstalk’s Field, Silo and Barn obligations, with transfers and claims on Base.

Under the normal allocation, 48.5% of newly minted Pinto goes to the regular Silo and 48.5% to the regular Field. The remaining 3% shifts from the budget to Beanstalk repayments as supply crosses 1 billion Pinto: initially 1% each to the repayment Field, Silo and Barn. Once the Barn is repaid, the repayment Field and Silo each receive 1.5%; once the Silo is repaid, the repayment Field receives 3%.

  • Field: Represent claims as transferable Plots in a separate repayment Field. Holders can transfer their positions and harvest Pinto as their Pods become harvestable.
  • Silo: Represent claims as transferable urBDV tokens, which earn Pinto repayments whether held in a wallet or Internal Balance. Earned Pinto stays with the account that earned it when tokens are transferred; future repayments follow the tokens. Claims can be paid to a chosen recipient’s wallet or Internal Balance. Holders must retain a positive urBDV balance to claim, so they should claim earned Pinto before transferring their entire balance.
  • Barn: Represent claims as transferable Fertilizer positions. Holders can claim repaid Pinto to their wallet or Internal Balance. Before Fertilizer moves, any accrued Pinto rewards for the sender and recipient are paid to each person’s own Internal Balance. The Fertilizer position and its future repayment rights then pass to the recipient.
  • Contract accounts: Eligible contracts that can act from their own address on Base can claim directly and choose where to receive their positions. At launch, contracts that needed to choose a receiver from Ethereum could not do so: the messenger targeted the wrong Base address. A separate upgrade is planned to restore that route. A contract receiving Fertilizer must support ERC-1155 transfers.

2. Convert Accounting and Penalties

Count each Convert’s use of the per-block, overall and per-Well capacity once, preserving the correct remaining capacity for later Converts in the same block. This capacity governs Grown Stalk penalties and is separate from Convert Up bonus capacity.

Make the overall penalty less sensitive to trades immediately before a Convert by measuring its effect against a reserve baseline that limits sudden changes. Individual Well checks still use spot reserves.

Scale Grown Stalk penalties to the proportion of a Convert that moves away from peg or exceeds remaining capacity. The penalty reflects the Convert’s size and expected liquidity impact, rather than treating the raw reserve imbalance as an amount of deposit value to penalize. At most 100% of the output PDV is subject to this penalty.

Apply these changes to ordinary and batch Pinto ↔ Well LP Converts and Pipeline Converts. Same-token Converts receive no penalty from this calculation. Existing Grown Stalk minimums, directional bonuses or penalties, and slippage protections still apply; invalid reserve or oracle data can prevent a Convert.

3. Referral Eligibility

When changing a delegate, clear the former delegate's eligibility only if their own recorded Sowing is below the eligibility threshold. Reject a zero-address delegate.

4. Plot Management

Add getters for Plot-index list length, a Plot's stored reverse index, selected array positions and half-open index ranges. Position queries preserve the requested order. Range queries require a nonempty range within the list bounds.

Add combinePlots for at least two positive caller-owned Plots supplied in ascending, exactly adjacent Pod Line order. Aggregate their Pods into the first Plot, remove later Plots from account indexing, and cancel listings for those later Plots. The first Plot's listing remains. Index removal uses swap-and-pop, so account-list order is not preserved.

Rationale

1. Beanstalk Repayments

Give Beanstalk holders a way to receive the repayments Pinto committed to at launch. Transferable positions let holders pass future repayment rights to someone else, while keeping track of Pinto already earned. Separate Field, Silo and Barn positions preserve the different claims holders had in Beanstalk. Repayment limits prevent paying more than is owed or using more newly minted Pinto than allocated.

2. Convert Accounting and Penalties

Later Converts should not face higher Grown Stalk penalties because earlier Converts were counted more than once. Correcting that accounting preserves the capacity actually available in each block.

Penalties should reflect how much a Convert moves Pinto away from peg or exceeds available capacity. Making the calculation less sensitive to preceding trades and scaling it to the Convert’s size makes the penalty better reflect that Convert’s effect.

3. Referral Eligibility

Delegation changes should preserve eligibility earned through an account’s own Sowing. Rejecting the zero address prevents assigning it referral eligibility.

4. Plot Management

Combining Plots simplifies management without moving their Pod Line position or creating Pods.

Function Changes

  • Added separate repayment plan getters returning (points,cap). Updated setShipmentRoutes to normalize zero planner addresses to the Diamond before storage and ShipmentRoutesSet emission. Added Silo/Barn receiving callbacks. A callback failure skips its transfer, while a later token-transfer failure or an invalid Field receipt can revert the outer operation.
  • Added SiloPayback receipt, reward-checkpoint, claim and balance getters. Added Barn repayment and Fertilizer claim/transfer accounting. Explicit Silo and Barn claims use the active Tractor publisher when present, otherwise the caller. Added direct contract-account claims and authenticated L1 receiver-record assignment. Receiver assignment requires caller 0x4200000000000000000000000000000000000007 and cross-domain sender 0xD2abd9a7E7F10e3bF4376fb03A07fca729A55b6f. It can overwrite the receiver’s record and requires a separate claim.
  • Added pre-transfer hook registration/encoding/call helpers and hasTokenHook/getTokenHook getters. Registration requires nonzero token, target and selector values, deployed target code and a successful test call with zero-valued arguments. The production facet's external hook-administration methods are commented out.
  • Updated shared capacity and penalty logic for ordinary, batch and Pipeline Converts through the new linked implementations. Added a calculateStalkPenalty overload with final fromAmount argument, selector 0x9752afb3. The old selector 0xb325d2ef is not removed or replaced by this cut. Historical routing confirms the old selector remained at 0x334a110ad41fd417d4c8c3f82d9c85a3850192be immediately before and after PI-15. The six-signed-integer input tuple retains its types and order. Its final two entries now represent capped and shadow overall deltaB. Of the four unchanged return types, the last three report current-call capacity deltas. The new simulation requires both initial and resulting Pinto reserves to be at least 10 Pinto, valid USD-oracle ratios at lookback zero, and successful Well Function calls.
  • Updated delegateReferralRewards to preserve independently earned former-delegate eligibility and reject zero-address delegation. The threshold is unchanged.
  • Added the four Plot-index query helpers and combinePlots. Moved Plot insertion into internal LibDibbler.insertPlot for shared use. A raw reverse-index lookup is not an existence check, and removed indexes receive type(uint256).max.

Event Changes

  • Added repayment-position minting and reward events, including UnripeBdvTokenMinted, SiloPaybackRewardsReceived, BarnPaybackRewardsReceived and SiloPaybackRewardsClaimed. Earlier population transactions emitted one RepaymentPlotAdded event for each of the 7,598 Plots. Fertilizer claims use ClaimFertilizer, and its transfers use ERC-1155 events. Each owner Fertilizer mint emits two TransferSingle logs, from the helper and caller, for one mint.
  • Added AddedTokenHook and TokenHookCalled on registration and successful calls. The internal removal helper emits RemovedTokenHook if called. The PI-15 finalization transaction emitted no such event. Existing Receipt uses the new Silo/Barn recipient values. Shipped records the shipping input, not a verified sum of every recipient's successful receipts.
  • Added PlotsCombined(account,fieldId,plotIndexes,totalPods). Canceling later Plot listings retains the existing cancellation events. No new Convert-capacity event or referral event is added by the reviewed source changes.
